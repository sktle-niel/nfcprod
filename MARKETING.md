# MARKETING.md — Go-to-Market Strategy

Goal: get business owners to **buy** the NFC cards and **keep using** the menu website.
Scope: Philippines, peso pricing, menu and review cards only (no payments or ordering in the app).
Numbers marked **(assumption)** are starting guesses to test, not facts.

## 1. Who we sell to

| Priority | Segment | Why they buy | Entry offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cafés, milk tea, juice bars, bakeries | Heavy phone use, many new customers, reviews drive foot traffic, menu changes often | Review cards, then website |
| 2 | Casual dining, carinderia, resto-bars | Printed menus get outdated and cost money to reprint | Website package |
| 3 | Small fine dining | Wants an elegant digital menu and reservations link | Website package |
| 4 | **Any local service business** (salon, barbershop, clinic, gym, auto shop) | Wants more Google and Facebook reviews. No menu needed | Review cards only |

Segment 4 is the fastest door-opener: the review cards work for any business, so the market is much larger than menu businesses. Every review-card buyer who also has a menu is a website upsell.

## 2. Offer ladder (land and expand)

```
₱580   1 NFC card          entry, impulse buy
₱780   Any 2 cards         "just add one more"
₱1,399 All 3 cards         best value, the anchor
₱1,799 NFC + Website       flagship, recurring need (menu changes)
```

Included in every offer: **free setup and guide** (sales point #1: "no work for you").

Recommendations:
1. **Fix the saving claim.** "Save ₱779" on All 3 cards does not match the price list (3 × ₱580 = ₱1,740, so the real saving is ₱341). Use the real number or explain what the ₱779 compares to. A wrong claim hurts trust and can be treated as misleading.
2. **Add one combo** for owners who have a menu: website package + all 3 cards. Suggested starting point: about ₱2,799 **(assumption, test it)** versus ₱3,198 bought separately. Having one clear "get everything" option raises the average order.
3. Keep **All 3 cards** as the visual "best value" so the ₱1,799 package feels reasonable beside it.
4. Check margins before any promo: card cost, domain cost, setup time, reseller commission. "Free hosting + domain" must still leave profit after year 1. Decide the renewal price now (domain renews yearly) so there are no surprises later.

## 3. Message

One promise: **"Tap. Menu opens. No app."** (already the headline).

| Audience | Pain | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Café / resto owner | Reprinting menus, wrong prices on paper | "Change a price once. Every customer sees it now." |
| Owner wanting visibility | Few reviews, few follows | "Customers tap, review in 10 seconds." |
| Busy owner | No time, not techy | "We set it up. Free. You get a simple guide." |
| Skeptic | Another gadget that won't be used | "Try the demo card on your own phone first." |

Rules: short sentences, peso prices visible, real photos of real cards on real counters, Taglish in chat and ads, English on the website.

## 4. Channels (best first)

### 4.1 Resellers (main engine, already in the plan)
Card sellers promote the platform and set up their own customers. Their work is our distribution.
- Give each reseller a **referral code or link** (`?ref=<code>`) so sales are traceable in the app.
- Pay outside the app (decided). Pick a simple, stated rule, for example a fixed amount per paid setup **(assumption)**. Pay on completion, not signup.
- Give them a **reseller kit**: demo card, 1-page pitch, price card, short video of the tap, the setup checklist, a Messenger reply template.
- Start with 3 to 5 trusted resellers, learn what works, then recruit more. Quality over quantity: bad setups hurt the brand.

### 4.2 Walk-in "tap demo" (highest close rate for local shops)
1. Visit during off-peak hours with a demo card.
2. Owner taps it and sees a menu on their own phone.
3. Offer: "I'll build your menu for free first. If you like it, you buy the card."
4. Build their menu from their Facebook page or menu photos, send the link that day.
5. Seeing **their own menu** on **their own phone** is the close.

### 4.3 Social (Facebook first, then TikTok and Instagram Reels)
- 15-second videos: tap, menu opens, owner edits a price, change appears.
- Before and after: paper menu versus tap menu.
- Short customer clips: "Tapped, reviewed, got a free coffee" only if the free item is **not** tied to a positive review (see compliance below).
- Join local business and food groups: answer questions first, pitch second.
- Run a simple Messenger flow: "Menu or reviews?" then quote.

### 4.4 Partnerships
Coffee and equipment suppliers, POS and printer vendors, signage shops, packaging suppliers, business associations, coworking and mall tenant offices. They already talk to your buyers. Offer them a referral reward.

### 4.5 Referral between owners
"Refer a friend: both of you get a free extra card or setup upgrade." **(assumption, price the reward first)**

## 5. Sales process

| Stage | Action | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Find | List 100 local targets (map search, social pages) | Warm list |
| Contact | Messenger or walk-in with the tap demo | Reply or meeting |
| Demo | Their menu on their phone | "I want this" |
| Quote | Show the ladder, recommend one | Decision |
| Setup | Free setup in 1 day (promise) | Live menu |
| Activate | Card in hand, test tap together | First real customer tap |
| Grow | Day 7 check-in, then upsell | Review cards to website, or the reverse |

### Common objections (short answers)

| They say | Answer |
|---|---|
| "May Facebook page na kami." | "Facebook is slow to find items. Tap goes straight to your menu, and you can link Facebook too." |
| "Mahal." | "It's less than printing and reprinting menus. One-time card price, free setup." |
| "Hindi techy ang customers." | "No app, no sign-up. Just tap, or scan the QR on the card." |
| "Paano kung walang NFC phone?" | "QR code is printed on the card for that." |
| "Sino mag-e-edit ng menu?" | "You, in a simple dashboard. And we guide you for free." |
| "May monthly fee ba?" | Have a clear, honest answer ready (decide renewal terms first). |
| "Paano kung mawala ang card?" | "The link stays the same, so a replacement card works the same." |

## 6. Launch plan

**Days 1 to 30 (prove it)**
- Finish the landing page and a **demo menu** (`/m/demo`).
- Replace the placeholder "Get started" link with a real contact path (Messenger or Viber link or an inquiry form).
- Recruit 3 resellers. Sign up 10 pilot businesses **(assumption)**. Build their menus free.
- Collect photos, short quotes and tap videos from the pilots (with permission).

**Days 31 to 60 (repeat it)**
- Turn the best pilots into case studies ("Café X: 40 reviews in 30 days" only with real numbers).
- Start paid reseller payouts. Run Facebook posts and Reels weekly.
- Write down the setup checklist and train new resellers from it.

**Days 61 to 90 (scale it)**
- Open partnerships (suppliers, vendors).
- Launch the owner-to-owner referral reward.
- Aim for the first 50 clients. Track what each channel costs per sale.

## 7. Retention (so they renew and refer)

- First menu live within 24 hours of payment.
- Test tap together on delivery. Take a photo of the card in place.
- Day 7: "Any prices to change?" Day 30: seasonal menu refresh.
- Monthly tip: new promo banner, new item, holiday hours.
- Easy path to add the second and third cards, and the website, later.
- Watch for churn signs: no menu edits for 60 days, no taps.

## 9. Rules to follow (compliance and trust)

- **Do not offer rewards for positive reviews**, and do not steer unhappy customers away from reviewing. Google and Facebook restrict incentivized or filtered reviews. The card should lead straight to the normal review page.
- Do not show "Save" numbers or customer results you cannot prove.
- Only use customer names, logos and photos with permission.
- The cards in the marketing images show a **third-party brand ("powered by Digifeel.io")**. Replace or remove it before publishing, and check the right to use those images and the Instagram, Facebook and Google logos in ads.
- Collect only what is needed from owners (email), and add the Privacy Policy and Terms before launch.
